# Audit pass

Decision, 2026-10-16. Not built.

A personal case and a company case share one record a stranger can check. The house keeps the case. Outside gets a control tree: proof that a decision happened and was not rewritten. A link opens that tree. The API returns the same object. There is no public index of houses or cases.

The cabinet `GET /actions/:id` stays the private case. Do not publish that payload as the pass.

## Outside

- Who proposed and who objected, as the role labels the court already sees. Not keys, accounts, or addresses of people.
- Outcome: `allow`, `deny`, or `escalate`, and a later human yes or no if someone answered.
- Constitution snapshot the court read.
- Hashes of the accepted writes. Not the bodies.
- Court transaction.
- Times of those steps.

## Stays in the house

Request text, justification, evidence, objection text, counter-advice, and the permitted payload. Mail, Telegram, and decide links. Agent keys, sign secrets, and prompt text. Payment and credit details.

A stranger can see the decision. A stranger does not receive the case.

## Not this

- Not a second product or a second court for companies. One protocol.
- Not legal force of a verdict in the outside world.
- Not a feed of every house.
